# Privacy Policy for LexiLadder: English Vocab

**Effective Date:** October 26, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 26, 2026  

Welcome to **LexiLadder: English Vocab** ("Application", "we", "our", or "us"). This application is developed by **dearyoti** as a free/freemium ad-supported educational application. This document informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Application.

By downloading, accessing, or using LexiLadder, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Offline-First Architecture & No Account Required

LexiLadder is designed with an **offline-first** philosophy. We respect your privacy and believe your learning progress belongs to you:

- **No Account Registration:** You are not required to create an account, register, or provide personal details (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to use any of the core study features.
- **Local Data Storage:** All your study progress, including mastered words, quiz results, test scores, flashcard bookmarks, and custom settings, is stored **100% locally on your device** using secure internal databases (SQLite/Room and DataStore). None of your personal study logs are transmitted or stored on our servers.

---

## 2. Information Collection and Use

While the Application itself does not collect personally identifiable information directly, it utilizes reputable third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or your device:

- **Device Information:** Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / IDFA).
- **Network & Diagnostic Data:** IP address, network connection state, diagnostic logs, and application performance metrics.

---

## 3. Third-Party Service Providers

The Application integrates third-party service providers that process data under their own privacy policies:

### A. Google Play Services
Used to provide core Android platform capabilities, license verification, and runtime support.  
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### B. Google AdMob (Advertising)
In the free version of LexiLadder, advertisements (such as banner ads and transition interstitial ads) are served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers to serve relevant ads according to user consent and regional regulations.  
- **Strict Learning Policy:** Advertisements are *never* displayed during active quiz questions, flashcard interactions, or level mastery exams to preserve an uninterrupted study environment.  
- [Google AdMob Privacy & Policy](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543?hl=en)

### C. Google Play In-App Billing
If you purchase the **LexiLadder Premium** upgrade (one-time purchase to permanently remove advertisements), the transaction is processed entirely and securely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive, process, or store your credit card details, bank information, or billing address.  
- [Google Payments Privacy Notice](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### D. Remote Content Updates (GitHub)
When checking for new vocabulary catalogs or language updates, the Application makes standard HTTPS requests to public static files on GitHub. No user identification or personal data is transmitted during content catalog downloads.  
- [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

---

## 4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy Compliance)

LexiLadder is an educational vocabulary learning application suitable for learners of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. In compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy, our application does not request personal registration, and third-party advertising partners are configured to comply with applicable children's privacy standards.

If you are a parent or guardian and you believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so that we can take necessary actions.

---

## 5. Data Retention & User Data Deletion

Because all study data is stored locally on your device, you have complete and absolute control over your data:

- You can reset or wipe all learning data at any time by going to your device's **Settings > Apps > LexiLadder > Storage > Clear Data**.
- Uninstalling the Application will permanently delete all locally stored databases, preferences, and progress.

---

## 6. Security

We value your trust in using our Application. We use industry-standard HTTPS encryption for downloading vocabulary packages and rely on the security of the Android sandboxed storage model to safeguard your local learning data.
