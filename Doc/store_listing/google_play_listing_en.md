## 3. Full Description (Max 4,000 characters)

Master English vocabulary one step at a time with **LexiLadder**! Whether you are preparing for everyday conversations, exams, or professional growth, LexiLadder makes vocabulary acquisition effortless, structured, and lasting—completely offline.

### 🌟 Why Choose LexiLadder?

- **🪜 Structured Level Progression:**  
  Advance through clear, CEFR-aligned vocabulary levels starting from foundational English (Level A1). Each level breaks vocabulary down into focused, high-yield thematic units.

- **⏱️ 5-Minute Daily Habit:**  
  Designed for busy lives. Just 5 minutes of focused daily practice builds rock-solid memory without overwhelming you.

- **🧠 Smart Spaced Repetition (SM-2):**  
  Never forget a word. Our adaptive spaced-repetition algorithm automatically identifies words you struggle with and schedules targeted reviews right before you forget them.

- **🏆 Level Mastery Tests:**  
  Prove your knowledge! Each level features a rigorous 10-question mastery test evaluating word definitions, reverse translations, and contextual usage. Score 80% or higher to unlock the next level and earn your badge.

- **✈️ 100% Offline-First:**  
  No internet? No problem. All core vocabulary, flashcards, quizzes, and mastery tests are stored locally on your device. Learn on the subway, on flights, or in remote areas.

- **🔄 Modular Content Updates:**  
  Receive new vocabulary packages, translations, and learning levels seamlessly without needing heavy APK updates.

- **🔒 Privacy by Design:**  
  No mandatory account registration, no passwords, and no email collection. Your learning progress stays 100% private on your own device.
